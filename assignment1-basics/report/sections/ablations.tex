\problem{layer\_norm\_ablation}{Remove RMSNorm and train}{1}

\textbf{Shared setup.}
All ablations used fresh TinyStories runs at 40.96M tokens, batch 16,
seed 42, pre-RMSNorm/RoPE/SwiGLU, and fixed seed-42 validation windows.
LR was $10^{-3}/10^{-4}$ except for no-RMSNorm. Other settings and
short stability probes are in Appendix~\ref{app:diagnostics}.

\begin{table}[htbp]
    \centering
    \small
    \begin{tabular}{@{}lrrrr@{}}
        \toprule
        Model & Peak LR & Parameters & Final val loss & $\Delta$ vs. baseline \\
        \midrule
        Baseline & $10^{-3}$ & 22,696,448 & \textbf{1.5879} & --- \\
        No RMSNorm & $5\times10^{-4}$ & 22,691,840 & 1.6598 & $+0.0719$ \\
        Post-norm & $10^{-3}$ & 22,696,448 & 1.6042 & $+0.0163$ \\
        NoPE & $10^{-3}$ & 22,696,448 & 1.7010 & $+0.1131$ \\
        SiLU & $10^{-3}$ & 22,827,520 & 1.6106 & $+0.0227$ \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
    \caption{Matched-budget ablation results at step 10,000. Lower validation
    cross-entropy is better. All runs use one seed; no-RMSNorm also changes LR.}
    \label{tab:ablation_results}
\end{table}

Removing all RMSNorms caused early loss explosions at peak LR $10^{-3}$.
A fresh run at $5\times10^{-4}$ remained stable but ended at 1.6598,
versus baseline 1.5879. Normalization supported the larger tested LR;
the 0.0719 loss gap also reflects LR retuning, not normalization alone.

\problem{pre\_norm\_ablation}{Implement post-norm and train}{1}

I moved block RMSNorms after residual additions, retaining the final norm.
Post-norm trained stably but ended at 1.6042 versus pre-norm's 1.5879,
and was worse at 98/100 evaluations. Pre-norm was modestly better under
the shared schedule; this does not establish inherent post-norm instability.

\problem{no\_pos\_emb}{Implement NoPE}{1}

Removing RoPE retained the causal mask and parameter count. NoPE learned
stably but was worse at all 100 evaluations, ending at 1.7010 versus
1.5879 (gap 0.1131, the largest tested ablation gap). This supports RoPE
at context 256 under this recipe, not a claim about length extrapolation.

\problem{swiglu\_ablation}{SwiGLU vs.\ SiLU}{1}

I replaced gated SwiGLU ($d_{\mathrm{ff}}=1344$) with two-projection SiLU
($d_{\mathrm{ff}}=2048$), approximately matching FFN parameters. Total
parameters increased by 0.58\% (Table~\ref{tab:ablation_results}). SiLU
trained stably but ended at 1.6106 versus 1.5879, and was worse at every
matched evaluation. Gating helped in this single-seed, shared-LR comparison.
